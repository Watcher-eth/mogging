YouTube Publisher Privacy Notice

This notice applies to the local Mogging YouTube Publisher used by the owners of MogLabs and Morphius. The publisher uses YouTube API Services. The YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy also apply.

What the publisher accesses

When an owner connects a channel, Google asks for permission to upload videos and read the channel and video information needed to verify the destination, avoid duplicates and confirm an upload or its schedule. Google handles sign-in. The publisher does not collect Google account passwords.

The publisher sends the selected video, title, description, audience and disclosure settings, and requested publish time to YouTube. It does not read comments or messages or send YouTube account information to the video or speech providers.

Storage and use

The publisher stores a refresh credential on the owner's computer in a folder limited to that owner. It records YouTube video IDs and delivery status locally for release tracking and duplicate prevention. This data is not sold or used for advertising.

Revoke access or request deletion

Use the publisher's disconnect command or visit Google Account permissions. Disconnect removes the local credential and cached YouTube data for that channel. If you revoke access on Google's page, the publisher clears its local credential and cached YouTube data when it detects the revoked authorization.

Disconnecting does not remove previously uploaded or scheduled videos. You can manage those videos in YouTube Studio. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email gabe@mogging.com.
