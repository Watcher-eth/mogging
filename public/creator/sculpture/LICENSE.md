# Classical sculpture assets

The shipping male and female sculptures are adapted from SMK – National Gallery of Denmark's public-domain cast scans, released under CC0.

Male: cast of the head of Michelangelo's David, SMK KAS2232.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Michelangelo_Buonarroti,_Hoved_fra_statuen_af_David,_,_KAS2232,_Statens_Museum_for_Kunst,_3D_model.stl

Female: Venus / Venere Italica, SMK KAS793.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ubekendt,_Venus,_Venere_Italica,_,_KAS793,_Statens_Museum_for_Kunst,_3D_model.stl

Adaptations: cropped to a bust, normalized and oriented, removed scanning chatter, preserved carved facial and hair detail, fitted surface anchors, added bounded shape-key pairs and neutral studio materials. These are museum sculptures, not scans of the named studio subjects or literal reconstructions of ancient Greek originals.

Stone textures are generated for this project. Relative controls remain illustrative, not personal measurements or treatment predictions.

`male-bone.glb` and `female-bone.glb` are fitted derivatives of the project's existing `/models/scattering-skull.glb` asset. The museum CC0 attribution above applies to the cast surfaces, not that separate source asset. Bone placement is illustrative rather than recovered anatomy.

## Reference-sheet busts

Classical curls, Textured crop, and Swept-back waves were reconstructed locally from supplied four-view sheets using TripoSR and fitted against the source silhouettes. The source sheets and reconstructed busts are separate from the museum CC0 assets. The corresponding bone models are illustrative fits of the same project skull.
