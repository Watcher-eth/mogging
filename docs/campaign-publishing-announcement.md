## 📢 Campaign rules update — one-week grace period

These publishing rules apply to **all Mogging campaigns**:

1. **Use the Mogging TikTok anchor** if your TikTok posting account has **1,000 or more followers**. [Open the anchor link](https://www.tiktok.com/t/ZS9UgQb9Cp1Eq-WbUok/#linkSource=DirectLink).
2. **Keep your Mogging link in your posting account’s bio** if that account has **1,000 or more followers**.

Posting accounts with **fewer than 1,000 followers are exempt from both requirements**.

### Grace period: until November 16 at 7:00 p.m. Pacific

You have one week to implement these changes. **Missing either link during this grace period will not reduce or deny an otherwise eligible payout.** Eligible videos submitted before the deadline remain protected if reviewed later.

After the deadline, the rules above apply to new submissions.

Find your Mogging bio link in [Accounts](https://www.mogging.com/creator/accounts). All other campaign rules, view thresholds and analytics requirements still apply.
