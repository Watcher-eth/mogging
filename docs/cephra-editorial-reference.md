# Cephra editorial motion reference

The new library uses gray instruments on white, lightly tinted, or dark paper. Measurements follow the mesh. Every shot derives its motion from elapsed video time so seeking and export produce the same frame. The reported view counts came from the shortlist supplied for the earlier study. They are historical snapshots.

| Reference and frame range | Observed sequence | New treatments and adaptations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Midface projection](https://www.tiktok.com/@cephra3/video/7704300623637810465), roughly 3–20s | The profile crop holds while a brow point resolves into a vertical datum. The nose base then gets its own horizontal relationship. | Datum drop, offset bridge, and support lattice. The lattice extends that construction to registered nose, lip and cheek points. |
| [Lens distortion](https://www.tiktok.com/@cephra3/video/7703220453979983137), roughly 5–27s | Camera framing stays comparable, local evidence arrives in small paired crops, and an elliptical sweep punctuates the contrast. | Focal reticle, perspective frustum, matched crops and rolling comparison. The frustum and split wipe adapt the perspective comparison without borrowing photos or scoring. |
| [Jaw angle](https://www.tiktok.com/@cephra3/video/7703892730501467424), roughly 3–26s | Two legs establish a pivot before the arc. A moving boundary reveals the registered mandible and the angle changes in place. | Hinge lock, arc envelope, and a soft highlight for the cutaway edge. |
| [Philtrum measurement](https://www.tiktok.com/@cephra3/video/7704787816353467680), roughly 2–10s | The nose base appears first, then the upper-lip endpoints establish the interval within the close crop. | Cupid fork and length transfer. The fork uses the registered lip center and scales its two marks to mouth width. |
| [Cheekbone height](https://www.tiktok.com/@cephra3/video/7702054345977777440), second half | Pupil and lip levels establish the coordinate system before the widest cheek location is shown. | Cheek coordinate and width transfer. Width transfer uses both mesh-registered cheek points when visible. |
| [Canthal tilt](https://www.tiktok.com/@cephra3/video/7700177887034461472) | Corner dots precede the level line, then slope becomes legible as the angle changes. | Eye-level reference and tilt wedge. The recorded eye sequence retains its authored timings. |

Two optional deformation curves extend the reference motion language. The two-stage curve pauses briefly before settling. The recoil curve moves slightly past the target before easing back. Both act only on an existing geometry change, with authored reveal and reset timings preserved.

Six added backgrounds are mesh, draft grid, dot field, depth grid, interference and studio halo. Patterns drift within the studio paper and dissolve through the existing shot handoff. The reference observations use saved contact sheets and targeted sequences, not a fresh audit of every decoded frame.
