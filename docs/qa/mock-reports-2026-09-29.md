# Creator Mock reports

CTA Studio now has separate CTA generator and Mock reports tabs sharing the existing local photo detection and alignment flow. Mock reports supports all eleven mobile categories, editable score and feature/measurement fields, the Eyes color palette, recommendations, and an iPhone viewport with a scroll-position control. Drafts survive tab switches and trips back to Photos.

The report painter follows `mogging-mobile/src/screens/report-screen.tsx` and uses its category data, action filtering, colors, card dimensions, hero crop, 150-point fade, score scale conversion, and 2.2-second potential shimmer. Geometry comes from the existing mobile overlay snapshot, resolved in the iPhone's logical viewport. Preview, PNG, and MP4 share one rendering path. Reduced-motion preview freezes animation, and hidden tabs stop painting.

Both generators use a shared random-score control: Low 1–2.5, Medium low 2.5–5, Medium high 5–7, High 7–9. Values have one decimal place; potential is at least current, and PSL is capped at 8.
