## E · kinetic infographic: retain, regroup, compress

The strongest new idea is that the same objects change their job as the argument develops. The first comparison becomes context for the later conclusion. New information does not require throwing away the whole screen.

| Approximate span | Visible state and motion | Transfer to Mogging |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–1.8 s | Two numeric/icon groups establish a comparison on near-black. Large values dominate; labels remain subordinate. | Two real feature scores, with one stable item identity per feature. White/ink and Mogging blue, not the source's gold. |
| 1.8–3.7 s | The comparison remains while local value/type emphasis changes attention. | Emphasize the spoken score or label, not every object at once. |
| 3.7–5.6 s | A question enters below the existing comparison with rise/defocus. | Reserve a headline lane; reveal its words on the narration clock without moving earlier words. |
| 5.6–12.0 s | A ruler/range proposition replaces the comparison. Long enough hold to read the graphic. | Native score ruler with real report value and fixed units. No invented thresholds or study results. |
| 12.0–14.8 s | A large count and repeated people grid introduce a set. | An explicitly illustrative feature-point constellation; count must come from the script. Never imply a research sample. |
| 14.8–16.3 s | The same marks divide into two groups rather than disappearing and respawning. | Stable `graphic/item:…/dot:N` nodes move into clusters on the spoken split/regroup cue. |
| 16.3–19.6 s | One large comparison card takes focus. | Give one idea a dominant frame, with a restrained surface and native face symbol. |
| 19.6–22.4 s | A second card supplies the contrast. | Paired cards, optional staggered vertical arrangement. |
| 22.4–25.8 s | Both cards shrink and move upward into a compact context rail. A program label and progress structure occupy the freed space. | Keep the exact score objects and their contents; scale their existing coordinate systems into a top rail, then introduce routine steps below. |
| 25.8–29.2 s | Local emphasis, progress and a concluding qualification complete the argument. | One clear conclusion/action; keep context quiet. Do not inherit the fitness intervention, study claim or branded pill. |

Measured activity windows in the supplied recording: 0.117–0.350, 1.817–2.150, 3.783–4.183, 5.685–5.918, 12.052–12.585, 14.820–15.487, 16.353–16.687, 19.620–19.820, 22.420–23.053, 25.822–26.088 and 27.022–27.988 seconds. They inform rhythm, not hard-coded script timings.

**Distinctive grammar:** comparison → question → scale → set → regroup → comparison → compact context + steps → conclusion. Large-to-small spatial continuity matters more than adding transition presets. Most of the screen is quiet at any instant; the object in motion carries the argument.

## Shared motion/layout contract

1. **One subject/object, one identity.** Real report components, portraits, graphic cards, labels, rulers and marks are named nodes in the same renderer. Moving a card also moves its own children. No screenshot patch or replacement digit layer.
2. **Retain context before adding content.** A comparison's `primary`/`secondary` cards shrink into the following sequence's context rail. Identical values do not restart counting or fade away during the handoff.
3. **One dominant action per beat.** Regroup marks, focus a tile, trace a contour, or reveal a sentence. Supporting content follows; unrelated decorative loops stay off by default.
4. **Space follows meaning.** A numeric scale has a large value and a readable ruler; comparison has two strong anchors; a routine is a large ordered list; image-free statements use the central stage. No phone-sized report floating in unused space.
5. **Continuity when possible, a clean cut otherwise.** `layout-morph` moves real shared graphic roots; `cinematic-flow` carries portrait/metric placements. Unrelated app/graphic screens use a restrained focus cut, not a false shared morph.
6. **The script owns time.** Sentence/cue identity determines beats. `caption-swish` follows aligned words when attached; a regroup can bind to a specific word. Without audio alignment, timing is an estimate, not claimed synchronization.
7. **Variation is constrained.** Seeds change arrangement and white/ink treatment, not factual scores, recommendations or narration. Existing report/overlay painters stay the owners of product visuals.

## Implemented authoring surface

The studio's **Visual format** selects App-led analysis, Portrait feature audit, Measurement explainer, Minimal kinetic explainer, or Explainer + app proof. **Variation** deterministically recompiles from the same script. Ten graphic layouts are editable: statement, comparison, scale, dial, calendar, story, constellation, pillars, sequence and evidence. White/ink/paper tone, values, labels, items, weekdays and illustrative dot count remain editable; each rendered component is targetable by the ordinary animation inspector. The later instrument and illustrated-story additions are detailed in [motion adapters](creator-animation-adapters.md).

Minimal kinetic explainer keeps complete sentence beats together and needs no photo. Explainer + app proof retains explicit open/scroll/report cues as actual editable reports. A comparison followed by a routine preserves its two cards in a compact rail. A point-map followed by a split/groups cue preserves the point identities and adds a word-bindable regroup track. This is a reusable starting vocabulary, not a claim of shot-for-shot reproduction of all reference edits or automatic natural-language understanding of arbitrary visual directions.

Validation exercises five recipes × twelve seeds, all ten native layouts, narration and score preservation, image-free painting, stable identities, shared card continuity and spoken regroup alignment. A future addition should extend this same schema/painter/track path and add coverage, rather than introduce a second renderer or a separate format-specific animation engine.
