# 📈 Meet Conversions: learn what works and earn more from it

Your new **Conversions** tab brings link clicks, downloads, conversions, and revenue into one place, with a breakdown by video.

Use it to spot which topics, hooks, and formats turn attention into action. Build on your strongest posts, test new approaches, and make your next video more effective.

High-converting videos can also earn a **5% commission with no fixed per-video commission cap**. Once verified directly attributed revenue reaches 150% of what we’ve paid for a video, the commission applies to **all verified directly attributed revenue**, not just the amount above that threshold. Keep your Mogging link in your bio and your code in the caption to qualify.

Shared-link estimates help you compare performance; verified direct attribution determines commission eligibility.

The aim is simple: give you more insight into your results and more control over how you improve them. Strong content can now earn beyond its view-based payout.
