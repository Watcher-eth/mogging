# 🏆 Go beyond your base payout

The new campaigns include five bonus opportunities:

## 🌍 Audience Excellence

![Audience Excellence globe badge](https://www.mogging.com/creator/achievements/audience-globe.png)

**Reward: $10 per qualifying video · up to five times per campaign**

- Reach **more than 100k views** on a video.
- Have **at least 45% Tier 1 audience** on that video.
- Earn up to **$50 total** from this bonus per campaign.

## 🔗 Link in Bio Bonus

![Link in Bio Bonus badge](https://www.mogging.com/creator/achievements/exclusive.png)

**Reward: an extra 5% of each eligible video’s payout · up to $10 per video**

- Keep your **Mogging link in your bio for the entire 30-day campaign**.
- The bonus is **paid after the campaign ends**.

## 🎯 Conversion Bonus

![Conversion Bonus target badge](https://www.mogging.com/creator/achievements/conversion-target.png)

**Reward: 5% of all verified directly attributed revenue once your video qualifies**

- Keep your **Mogging link in your bio** and your **code in the caption**.
- Your video qualifies when verified directly attributed revenue reaches **150% of its paid payout**.
- The 5% applies to **all qualifying revenue**, not just the amount above that threshold.
- There is **no fixed per-video commission cap**.

[Track your videos in Conversions →](https://www.mogging.com/creator/conversions)

## 🏅 Million View Club

![Million View Club badge](https://www.mogging.com/creator/achievements/million.png)

**Reward: $15 · one-time per campaign**

Have **three separate videos each reach 1M views** during the campaign.

## 💎 Decimillion Club

![Decimillion Club badge](https://www.mogging.com/creator/achievements/decimillion.png)

**Reward: $30 · one-time per campaign**

Have **ten separate videos each reach 1M views** during the campaign.

---

Track your progress on each campaign page. Bonuses are subject to verification and the campaign’s available bonus budget.
