Working diagnosis

The highest-value next question is whether viewers understand why they should purchase their own report. Entertainment reach, qualified intent, completed payment and recurring use are separate outcomes. Celebrity or comparison content may satisfy curiosity without generating personal buying intent; this is a hypothesis, not a conclusion from an audited sample of all videos.

Native ordered new-install funnel: 2,201 opens → 1,635 authenticated → 1,491 selected photos → 1,466 paywalls → 719 purchase starts → 39 completed purchases. Linked-attempt outcomes are 762 cancellations, 87 unresolved, 43 purchased/activated, 23 errors and 1 pending. Errors matter, but repairing them alone cannot account for the cancellation volume. Keep the UI work already completed and evaluate it with the correct cohort.

Minimal measurement contract

Reuse the existing events and ledgers. Carry creator/post click ID, anonymous/installation ID, account identity, flow ID, immutable release/entry mode and experiment assignment through checkout. StoreKit transaction or Stripe session owns payment truth; entitlement and completed analysis own activation truth. Add coarse platform/browser context where absent, including embedded browser versus Safari/Chrome. Distinguish automatic navigation, intentional taps, payment cancellation, app interruption, true errors and unresolved outcomes.

For creators, collect publication time and daily view snapshots so we measure views earned during a test. Native watch-time/retention data should establish how many viewers actually see the CTA. Use existing analytics evidence/OCR where suitable instead of rebuilding that pipeline.

Preserve unknown attribution for iOS rather than guessing. Apple's campaign links provide a provider-level cross-check; AppsFlyer deferred attribution is conditional and cannot identify every viewer or install. Do not introduce invasive matching to make a dashboard appear complete.

What to test in the creative

  • Personal relevance: the viewer understands what they will learn about their own features.
  • Product proof: show the actual report, an understandable measurement and what happens next; preserve the polished generator style.
  • Visible action: a legible Mogging.com CTA while attention is still present, consistent bio destination and closing.
  • Expectation match: do not advertise a free full evaluation or instantaneous transformation when the destination sells an evaluation/membership.
  • Audience fit: judge source/device/market by activated customers, not Tier 1 share or views alone.

Budget and decision rules

No overall spend increase is proposed. Ring-fence a small test allocation from the existing creator budget; $300–$500 is a suggested planning ceiling subject to actual creator rates and approval, not a committed spend. Do not retroactively change earned payouts. Run one primary acquisition experiment at a time rather than fragmenting a few dozen purchases across many variants.

Use 24–48-hour view/click snapshots for early creative diagnostics, a consistent seven-day click-to-first-purchase window for comparable paid outcomes, and later 30-day proceeds/contribution. Recent cohorts stay marked immature. Deduplicate repeat views/visits where possible and account for creator-level clustering. One versus two purchases cannot establish a winner; report uncertainty and extend the test if it remains underpowered.

A scale decision requires a functioning attributed path and improved activated customers or contribution, not just more views, clicks or App Store opens. Base acceptable CAC on realized proceeds minus evaluation/processing costs and a conservative renewal assumption, not active-access MRR.

Illustration, not a forecast

20 million future measured views × 0.05% qualified visit yield × 2% visitor-to-payer conversion = 200 buyers. At 0.20% visit yield and the same purchase conversion, it becomes 800 buyers. These are invented scenario assumptions, not benchmarks or current conversion rates. They show why improving the bridge from attention to relevant intent can matter as much as another paywall refinement.
